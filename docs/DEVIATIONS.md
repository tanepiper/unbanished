- [Deviation] [apps/game/src/ambience.rs:1](../apps/game/src/ambience.rs#L1): Map ambience placed by `FUN_1400a57c0` (see docs/STARTING-AUDIO-EMITTERS.md, docs/AUDIO.md): - one `Audio/Effects/Environment.rsc:ambient` per `int_150`-cell square region. Its AmbientEnvironmentComponent (`FUN_140043c70`) weighs the region's cells against the description's terrain classes (cell-type mask, optional entity template, weight), picks the heaviest, and feeds its warm and cold effects to the two emitter slots at `t` and `1 - t`, where `t` is the temperature's place in the description's fade range; - one `Audio/Effects/WindEffect.rsc:ambient` at height `float_170`. The river emitters (every `float_130` along the river paths) wait for the in-game water geometry (`Game +0x110`).

## `apps/game/src/shadow.rs`

- [Deviation] [apps/game/src/shadow.rs:1](../apps/game/src/shadow.rs#L1): The sun's shadow map (`Graphics::SunLight`), ported from Runtime-steam-x64.dll. See docs/SHADOWS.md. Each frame the sun fits an orthographic camera around the part of the ground the main camera sees (`SunLight::GetSunCamera` 0x1801098a0), renders the casters' depth into a square depth buffer (`SunLight::DrawShadowMap` 0x180109450), and the lit pixel shaders compare against it through `lc[10..13]`, the shadow projection set by `SunLight::Draw` 0x180109040. Deviation: the shadow detail (Medium, 4 taps) and resolution (1024²) are the `Video::Configuration` defaults; the Options dialog's choices are not wired up. Casters are not culled by the main camera's frustum as the original's sun draw group is (`BeginDrawGroup` with the main camera as cull camera), so objects just off screen still cast into view. The planar reflection samples the main view's shadow map rather than one fitted to its own camera.
