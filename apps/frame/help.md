# Playing Un-Banished

This window starts Un-Banished on your own copy of Banished. Nothing is copied: the game reads
the files where Steam installed them, or from a folder you choose.

1. **Settings, Game**: choose **Banished 1.0.7** to play exactly the original game with its own
   mods. Choose **Un-Banished** to play with the extensions: multiplayer, the mod sites,
   recordings and rule hooks. A build without extensions offers Banished only.
2. **Settings, Game files**: the copies of Banished found in Steam are listed. On a Mac or on
   Linux, Steam for Windows in CrossOver or Wine is searched too. To use another copy, choose
   **A folder I choose** and type the folder: the Banished folder, its `WinData` folder, or any
   folder with `data0.pkg`.
3. **Play**: press Start. The game opens its own window. Quit the game, or press Stop here, to
   come back.

::: tip
Your saves and the game's own settings stay where Banished keeps them: `Documents/Banished`.
:::

::: info
Resolution, full screen and the game's other options are in the game's own Options window, as
in the original.
:::
